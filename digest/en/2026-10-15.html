Write forward/backward quantization schemes as ψf,ψb\psi_f,\psi_bψf​,ψb​, and parameters/tokens as N,DN,DN,D. The empirical law separates effective capacity, effective data and persistent optimization error:[2]

L=[A(ρN(ψf)N)−α+B(ρD(ψb)D)−β]γ+ϵf(ψf)+ϵb(ψb)+EL=\left[A(\rho_N(\psi_f)N)^{-\alpha}+B(\rho_D(\psi_b)D)^{-\beta}\right]^\gamma+\epsilon_f(\psi_f)+\epsilon_b(\psi_b)+EL=[A(ρN​(ψf​)N)−α+B(ρD​(ψb​)D)−β]γ+ϵf​(ψf​)+ϵb​(ψb​)+E

Convex, differentiable-loss analysis with bounded second moments motivates nonvanishing forward-rounding and backward-gradient errors. With schemes fixed, the fitted law approaches E+ϵf+ϵbE+\epsilon_f+\epsilon_bE+ϵf​+ϵb​ as model and data grow.[2]
