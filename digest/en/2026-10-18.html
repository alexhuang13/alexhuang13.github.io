Same Policy, Different Trajectories

Consistency in long-horizon RL · Selectors and budgets · Reading NTK

This issue examines training–sampling consistency in long-horizon RL and the budget boundaries of selection and feedback in 3D generation. Five papers/blogs and one technical follow-up are overlap-window additions, alongside two version records with unverified changes; none is presented as first released today. Our classic is NTK: from Jacobians to spectral filtering.

Same weights, different policy

At matched data and compute, does tactile supervision help?

Spark-0: Scaling Spatially Aligned Tactile Pretraining for Robot Dexterous Manipulation ↗
Tianxing Chen · Wenwei Lin · Chuqiao Lyu et al.
First posted 2026-10-15 13:56 EDT · v1 · overlap-window addition

Using 1,000 hours of HumanTouch, retaining tactile pretraining supervision raises vision-only Ego Humanoid success from 52.5 to 71.7% against a same-data/compute baseline that discards touch. This tests supervision quality, not a fitted data-scaling exponent. Real-robot results also use deployment-time touch and have a different attribution.[6]

Spend extra inference budget at critical states

VeriVLA: Adaptive Test-Time Verification for Flow-Based VLAs with Latent World Models ↗
Xueli Sun · Yuxiang Lu · Yantong Liu · Xianzhe Fan · Xi Chen · Zhe Liu · Hengshuang Zhao
First posted 2026-10-15 10:34 EDT · v1 · overlap-window addition

Temporal variation and cross-sample variance from two initial action chunks trigger extra sampling, ranked by a JEPA latent world model. Mean completion score across three real tasks rises from 50.8 to 65.5%, adding 326 ms per triggered decision on an RTX4090. This is conditional latency, not a fixed per-step cost or full-task success rate.[8]
