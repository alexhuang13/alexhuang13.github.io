Data × learning rate · Muon initialization · Graph tests · Kaplan scaling laws

Three main reads connect joint schedules, initialization geometry, and moment criteria for graph statistics. Fourteen briefs cover Muon, scaling, and post-training blogs. The classic revisits Kaplan scaling laws to separate data, model, step, and parallelism budgets.

When good data enters depends on when the learning rate decays

Data Schedules and Learning Rate Schedules Should Be Designed Together ↗
Haodong Wen · Kairong Luo · Tingkai Yan · Wenguang Chen · Kaifeng Lyu
First posted 2026-10-21 03:42 EDT · v1; listed October 22

Let γ(t) be the mid-training (MT) fraction and η(t) the learning rate. With compute and MT tokens fixed, introducing MT earlier can win under decay and lose under constant LR. [1]

γ allocates data; R is the cumulative learning rate available to MT-specific features
min⁡0≤γ(t)≤1E LMT(θT),∫0Tγ(t) dt≤αT,R(s)=∫sTη(t)γ(t) dt\min_{0\le\gamma(t)\le1}\mathbb E\,L_{\rm MT}(\theta_T),\qquad \int_0^T\gamma(t)\,dt\le\alpha T,\qquad R(s)=\int_s^T\eta(t)\gamma(t)\,dt0≤γ(t)≤1min​ELMT​(θT​),∫0T​γ(t)dt≤αT,R(s)=∫sT​η(t)γ(t)dt

The explanation uses block-diagonal quadratics and additive gradient noise. Extract one MT-specific direction with curvature λ>0, initial value z₀, variance ν, and discrete-step interval h. [1]

One-direction form of the paper's linear SDE: bias followed by variance
dzt=−λη(t)γ(t)zt dt+η(t)hν dBt,EλzT22=λz022e−2λR(0)+hλν2∫0Tη(s)2e−2λR(s) dsdz_t=-\lambda\eta(t)\gamma(t)z_t\,dt+\eta(t)\sqrt{h\nu}\,dB_t,\qquad \mathbb E\frac{\lambda z_T^2}{2}=\frac{\lambda z_0^2}{2}e^{-2\lambda R(0)}+\frac{h\lambda\nu}{2}\int_0^T\eta(s)^2e^{-2\lambda R(s)}\,dsdzt​=−λη(t)γ(t)zt​dt+η(t)hν​dBt​,E2λzT2​​=2λz02​​e−2λR(0)+2hλν​∫0T​η(s)2e−2λR(s)ds

With constant η, spending the budget fixes R(0); placing MT last maximizes every R(s), minimizing variance. Under decay, moving MT earlier also raises R(0), creating a bias–variance trade-off. [1]

Evidence boundary

The SDE assumes fixed additive noise. Its power-law three-stage optimum requires further assumptions and long horizons; the decomposition alone does not establish an optimum for general LLM training. [1]

Muon's initialization geometry: a diagonal mean is not a diagonal trajectory

How Does Orthogonalization Adapt to the Neural Network Hessian Structure? A Gradient Self Outer-Product Analysis at Initialization ↗
Shenyang Deng · Garvit Goyal · Tianyu Pang · Shuhua Yu · Yaoqing Yang
First posted 2026-10-20 18:48 EDT · v1; listed October 22

The object is E[GGᵀ] over random initialization. It motivates compatibility with known row-block Hessian structure, without estimating Hessian values. [2]

Exact polar expression; †/2 denotes the pseudoinverse square root, while NS implementations are approximate
D=polar⁡(G)=(GG⊤)†/2G,HMuon=(GG⊤)1/2⊗ID=\operatorname{polar}(G)=(GG^\top)^{\dagger/2}G,\qquad H_{\rm Muon}=(GG^\top)^{1/2}\otimes ID=polar(G)=(GG⊤)†/2G,HMuon​=(GG⊤)1/2⊗I

For W∈Rᵈˣᵏ, G=(WWᵀ−M)W, fixed M⪰0, and independent N(0,1) weights, Wishart moments give the exact identity: [2]

Theorem 1: for fixed d and M, the leading diagonal is k³, with generic nonzero target off-diagonals of order k²
E[GG⊤]=a3I−2a2M+kM2,a2=k(k+d+1),a3=k3+3(d+1)k2+(d2+3d+4)k\mathbb E[GG^\top]=a_3I-2a_2M+kM^2,\quad a_2=k(k+d+1),\quad a_3=k^3+3(d+1)k^2+(d^2+3d+4)kE[GG⊤]=a3​I−2a2​M+kM2,a2​=k(k+d+1),a3​=k3+3(d+1)k2+(d2+3d+4)k
Evidence boundary

Off-diagonal cancellation in expectation proves neither operator-norm concentration of one Gram nor persistence during training. LLM tests use initialization statistics—25 initializations and 64 fixed sequences—not a theorem about stable spectral bases along training. [2]

When the third moment detects Gaussianity better than the fourth

Third and Fourth Moment Phenomena for Monochromatic Edges and Applications to Graph-Based Two-Sample Tests ↗

Graph two-sample tests count between-group edges in a pooled similarity graph. Under the Poissonized null, conditioning on locations gives independent Bernoulli(p) labels. Balanced and unbalanced proportions require different Gaussian criteria. [3]

HyMUON combines long-history averaging with a fast EMA

Hybrid Momentum Methods for Optimizing Deep Learning Models ↗
Haifeng Xu · Yijin Ren · Chenyu Zhou · Qi Deng · Dongdong Ge · Yinyu Ye
First October 20; v2 October 21 05:25 EDT; overlap-window addition

HyM blends long-history averaging with fast momentum; HyMUON then applies NS. LLaMA-1.3B/C4 at 10k steps gives validation loss 2.777 versus 2.903. HyMUON is tuned and evaluates averaged iterates; Muon uses previously selected settings and training iterates. The v2 note concerns author order/affiliations; no substantive new result is confirmed. [4]

Virtual-world CPT: diversity has limits for distant transfer

Matteo Benati · Alessandro Londei · Denise Lanzieri · Raffaello Mastromarino · Vittorio Loreto

Shawn Hu · Jake Ward · Aria Wong · Nathan Hu · Bryce Woodworth · Alexander Matt Turner · Alex Cloud

Flagged trajectories update only FORGET; unflagged ones update both adapters, with RETAIN-only anchor training. Removing FORGET gives near-zero hacking and nearly +20pp correctness over base in Qwen3-8B/Countdown-Code. RL uses handcrafted monitors and single turns; constructing anchors remains a limitation. ALTK-Evolve distills training-task outcomes into guidelines

ALTK-Evolve: How learned memory tops the τ²-bench leaderboard on customer service tasks without a frontier model ↗
Ashwath Vaithinathan Aravindan · Vatche Isahagian · Vinod Muthusamy · Jayaram Radhakrishnan · Evelyn Duesterwald et al.
Published October 20; overlap-window addition

GPT-4.1 extracts rules from training trajectories, then freezes them for held-out prompts without weight updates. Retail Pass^4 is 67.5%—all four trials succeed, not pass@4. The leaderboard snapshot is October 12, and longer guidelines increase token use. [17]

Kaplan scaling laws: identify the resource that limits loss

Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models ↗
Jared Kaplan · Sam McCandlish · Tom Henighan · Tom B. Brown · Benjamin Chess · Rewon Child · Scott Gray · Alec Radford · Jeffrey Wu · Dario Amodei
First 2020-01-22 22:59 EST (January 23 UTC); read v1; year refers to the technical report
Foundational scaling-laws work; OpenAlex record: 1,745 citations (checked 2026-10-22) ↗

After Transformers (2017) and GPT-2 (2019), the allocation question was larger models versus longer training. This work relates WebText2 token cross-entropy to non-embedding parameters N, data D, steps S, and tokens per step B. [18]

Each empirical fit requires the other resources not to bind; the three laws cannot simply be multiplied
L(N)≃(Nc/N)0.076,L(D)≃(Dc/D)0.095,L(Cmin⁡)≃(Cc/Cmin⁡)0.050L(N)\simeq(N_c/N)^{0.076},\qquad L(D)\simeq(D_c/D)^{0.095},\qquad L(C_{\min})\simeq(C_c/C_{\min})^{0.050}L(N)≃(Nc​/N)0.076,L(D)≃(Dc​/D)0.095,L(Cmin​)≃(Cc​/Cmin​)0.050
The joint fit recovers a single-resource law when one term dominates
L(N,D)=[(NcN)αN/αD+DcD]αDL(N,D)=\left[\left(\frac{N_c}{N}\right)^{\alpha_N/\alpha_D}+\frac{D_c}{D}\right]^{\alpha_D}L(N,D)=[(NNc​​)αN​/αD​+DDc​​]αD​
Learning curves are fitted after warmup; Sₘᵢₙ adjusts for batch size
L(N,Smin⁡)=(NcN)αN+(ScSmin⁡)αS,αN≃0.077,αS≃0.76L(N,S_{\min})=\left(\frac{N_c}{N}\right)^{\alpha_N}+\left(\frac{S_c}{S_{\min}}\right)^{\alpha_S},\qquad \alpha_N\simeq0.077,\quad\alpha_S\simeq0.76L(N,Smin​)=(NNc​​)αN​+(Smin​Sc​​)αS​,αN​≃0.077,αS​≃0.76
Actual FLOPs differ from adjusted minimum compute; the model includes parallelism efficiency
Bcrit(L)≃B∗L−1/αB,αB≃0.21,Smin⁡=S1+Bcrit/B,Cmin⁡=6NBS1+B/BcritB_{\rm crit}(L)\simeq B_*L^{-1/\alpha_B},\quad\alpha_B\simeq0.21,\qquad S_{\min}=\frac{S}{1+B_{\rm crit}/B},\qquad C_{\min}=\frac{6NBS}{1+B/B_{\rm crit}}Bcrit​(L)≃B∗​L−1/αB​,αB​≃0.21,Smin​=1+Bcrit​/BS​,Cmin​=1+B/Bcrit​6NBS​
Allocation exponents follow from the fitted training model, rather than guessing from three one-dimensional curves
αC=(αN−1+αB−1+αS−1)−1,N∝Cmin⁡αC/αN,B∝Cmin⁡αC/αB,Smin⁡∝Cmin⁡αC/αS\alpha_C=\left(\alpha_N^{-1}+\alpha_B^{-1}+\alpha_S^{-1}\right)^{-1},\qquad N\propto C_{\min}^{\alpha_C/\alpha_N},\quad B\propto C_{\min}^{\alpha_C/\alpha_B},\quad S_{\min}\propto C_{\min}^{\alpha_C/\alpha_S}αC​=(αN−1​+αB−1​+αS−1​)−1,N∝CminαC​/αN​​,B∝CminαC​/αB​​,Smin​∝CminαC​/αS​​
Evidence boundary

The exponents depend on data, tokenizer, optimizer, and budget adjustments; §6.3 identifies extrapolation contradictions. Chinchilla, read October 17, later revised allocation under a different protocol. [18]
