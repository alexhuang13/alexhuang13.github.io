Static accuracy and online reward quality are different objectives

Low-precision RL: three checks before treating implementations as one policy

vLLM Delivers Day-0 Support for MiniMax-M3.1-Flash ↗
Inferact and the vLLM Team
Technical blog published/updated October 23

The training section combines an NVFP4 checkpoint with veRL, FSDP2, and LoRA, freezing base experts and syncing adapters. Before training it checks weight transfer, expert-routing replay, and same-token logprobs: equal weights can still yield different execution.[2]

Explanatory notation for token-level IS; the source specifies a ratio cap of 2
wt=ptrainer(at∣ht)prollout(at∣ht),wˉt=min⁡{2,wt}w_t=\frac{p_{\rm trainer}(a_t\mid h_t)}{p_{\rm rollout}(a_t\mid h_t)},\qquad \bar w_t=\min\{2,w_t\}wt​=prollout​(at​∣ht​)ptrainer​(at​∣ht​)​,wˉt​=min{2,wt​}

Each batch receives one optimizer step, using rollout logprobs as old logprobs and skipping a recomputation forward pass. This condition does not directly transfer to multiple reuse epochs or more stale training.[2]

Evidence boundary

This is a working training case, not proof that cap=2 stabilizes every GRPO setup or that repetition collapse has a single cause.[2]

Muon, μP, and data scheduling in a 320B MoE recipe

IQuest-Q1: Advancing Foundation Capabilities for Agentic CLI Systems ↗
A. Jian Yang et al.
October 22, 05:01 EDT · v1

320B total / 15B active parameters; pretraining combines Stable SplitNorm, Muon, and μP, increasing the coding mixture later. The report emphasizes a margin between safe and optimal learning rates; it does not isolate Muon’s causal contribution.[3]

Joint scaling of continuous semantics and discrete generation

Cola2: Co-Adaptive Hierarchical Language Modeling with Continuous Semantic Compression and Balanced Discrete Generation ↗
Hongcan Guo · Yian Zhao · Feng Wang · Shen Nie · Guoqiang Wei · Yan Zeng · Hengshuang Zhao
October 22, 10:04 EDT · v1

Dynamic semantic segmentation lets token prediction update the encoder while stop-gradient decouples the flow prior. Experiments at about 210B tokens examine design and scaling; an unconverged model with 346.03B source tokens averages 60.10 over eight tasks. Cross-model token differences do not establish a compute-matched advantage.[4]

Data sources: control priors without robot trajectories

Learning Robot Behavior Priors from Non-Robotic Synthetic Data ↗
Mateusz Odrowaz-Sypniewski et al.
October 22, 09:43 EDT · v1

Three procedural control abstractions train a Transformer with nontrivial zero-shot action prediction on unseen robot tasks. A compute-matched real-data baseline is stronger only on its training datasets. This compares data sources and transfer, not a universal robot scaling law.[5]

Data quality and trainable parameters at fixed token budgets

AQL-Lite: Enabling Efficient Knowledge Acquisition for Arabic LLMs via Data-Centric Continual Pretraining ↗
Houssam Eddine Boukhalfa · Mourad Bouache · Mourad Dahmane
October 21, 15:04 EDT · v1

At a matched 1B tokens, curated data improves the four-task mean by 3.51 points over raw web. At 5B tokens, partial-parameter training recovers 94% of full-CPT gain in 52% of wall time; means are 73.19 vs 73.58. Recovery is the incremental gain over base, not retention of 94% of all capabilities.[6]

VANE: next-frame prediction in a fixed semantic space

Learning from Next-Frame Prediction: Towards Unified and Scalable Visual Representation Learning ↗
Yifeng Yao et al.
October 22, 00:46 EDT · v1

With a frozen semantic tokenizer, a causal encoder predicts the next full-frame embedding and an auxiliary decoder preserves detail. The paper reports model/data scaling and a 7.1-point gain over V-JEPA 2.1 in EPIC-KITCHENS-100 anticipation mean-class recall@5; strict budget matching is not specified in the abstract.[7]

Scene coverage and data scaling for closed-loop navigation

AirPlayground: Benchmarking Aerial Navigation across Diverse Reconstructed Environments ↗
Rangya Zhang et al.
October 21, 09:13 EDT · v1

94 reconstructed scenes, 94K demonstrations, and 46.5M observation-state-action samples. Scaling ACT training from 5 to 70 scenes raises seen/unseen median success from 30.6% to 41.2% and 16.8% to 29.4%. Offline prediction error is not interchangeable with closed-loop success.[8]

Choose what to retain before compressing history

Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces ↗
Albert Gu · Tri Dao
First posted 2023-12-01; COLM 2024; read v2, 2024-05-31
COLM 2024 Outstanding Paper Award ↗

S4 (2022) compresses history with a fixed linear system; linear attention (2020) accumulates a finite state. Which language tokens deserve memory depends on content. Mamba (2024) makes writing, reading, and forgetting input-dependent.[10]

N=1,A=−1,B=1,Δt=log⁡(1+ezt)⟹ht=(1−gt)ht−1+gtxt,gt=σ(zt)N=1,\quad A=-1,\quad B=1,\quad\Delta_t=\log(1+e^{z_t})\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_t=(1-g_t)h_{t-1}+g_tx_t,\quad g_t=\sigma(z_t)N=1,A=−1,B=1,Δt​=log(1+ezt​)⟹ht​=(1−gt​)ht−1​+gt​xt​,gt​=σ(zt​)

A small gate retains state and ignores the current input; a large gate resets toward it. This scalar interpretation is not a convergence theorem for the full network. Input-dependent coefficients break fixed convolution but preserve associativity of affine composition.[10]

Equivalent algebraic explanation of scan; associativity follows from affine composition
Ft(h)=at⊙h+bt,(a2,b2)∘(a1,b1)=(a2⊙a1, a2⊙b1+b2)F_t(h)=a_t\odot h+b_t,\qquad (a_2,b_2)\circ(a_1,b_1)=(a_2\odot a_1,\ a_2\odot b_1+b_2)Ft​(h)=at​⊙h+bt​,(a2​,b2​)∘(a1​,b1​)=(a2​⊙a1​, a2​⊙b1​+b2​)

A parallel prefix scan is therefore possible. The implementation fuses discretization and recurrence in SRAM and recomputes states in backward, avoiding the full B×L×D×N state tensor in HBM. Selection and hardware-aware execution jointly support linear sequence complexity.[10]

Evidence boundary

Read §2, §§3.1–3.5, Theorem 1, and the selective-copy/induction setups in §4.1. Finite state remains an information bottleneck; linear complexity does not establish equivalence to attention on every retrieval task. Question: at fixed state dimension, can we characterize how much information content selection can retain?[10][11]
