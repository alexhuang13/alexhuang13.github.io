Filtering tasks; tracking the policy behind each token

Post-Training at the Frontier ↗
Ching-Chia Wang · Eddy Nassif · Aastha Jhunjhunwala
Published October 22, 2026; newly included from overlap window

For search agents, filtering and rollout synchronization jointly determine the update data. This October 22 blog is newly included: eight probes per question, then SFT on 4,932 traces and GRPO on 1,480 tasks.[1]

Hot-Load preserves requests and caches across weight changes. Strict on-policy operation can reset prompt caches and wait for all replicas. The equation is not an implemented correction; cached execution need not equal recomputation under new weights.[1]

Evidence boundary

The fastest delta Hot-Load is about 15× the average cold start, measuring weight updates only. No unbiased mixed-version gradient guarantee is established.[1]
