MuonGS: Pretraining LLMs with Relaxed Weight-Norm Control ↗
Athanasios Glentis · Dawei Li · Mingyi Hong
First posted 2026-10-27 · v1

Let r_t be the Frobenius weight norm and O_t a Muon direction with norm at most √p, p=min(m,n). The paper studies entire trajectories rather than a single normalized step. Its accumulated effective learning rate bounds relative displacement over any later interval.[1]

The claimed ≈15% longer-horizon gain is a fitted token-equivalent speedup over MuonH, not measured wall-clock acceleration. Bounded norm is sufficient, but not necessary, for sustained accumulation; momentum-free convergence additionally assumes a scale-invariant smooth objective.[1]

Data composition moves the ceiling

Scaling Laws for Tool-Use Agents via Controllable Environment Synthesis ↗
Andrew Szot · Samira Abnar · Omar Attia · Abbas Kazerouni · Michael Kirchhof · Cheng-Yu Hsieh · Raviteja Vemulapalli · Alexander Toshev
First posted 2026-10-27 · v1

SynthAPI separates apps A, tasks per app T, profiles per task S, and profile complexity D. Following token-based scaling (Kaplan 2020; Chinchilla 2022, background), it models normalized downstream success from environment composition; T here counts tasks, not optimizer steps.[2]

Empirical fit, separately by model size M; σ is logistic [2]
N=ATS,P^M(A,T,S,D)=σ(c0+cAlog⁡2(ASD)) σ(b0+bTlog⁡2T)νN=ATS,\quad \widehat P_M(A,T,S,D)=\sigma(c_0+c_A\log_2(ASD))\,\sigma(b_0+b_T\log_2T)^\nuN=ATS,PM​(A,T,S,D)=σ(c0​+cA​log2​(ASD))σ(b0​+bT​log2​T)ν

P averages gains over the base model after benchmark-specific normalization by the best observed gain. It is not raw accuracy. More apps and database diversity raise the fitted ceiling; tasks per app control approach to it.[2]

Held-out top 25% of training FLOPs, normalized score [2]
MAEcomposition=0.0285,MAEquantity=0.1706\mathrm{MAE}_{\rm composition}=0.0285,\qquad\mathrm{MAE}_{\rm quantity}=0.1706MAEcomposition​=0.0285,MAEquantity​=0.1706
Boundary

The study trains 849 models across four sizes. Equal task count does not imply equal token, synthesis, or execution cost; the fitted law is not a universal composition theorem.[2]

MuSAM inserts a SAM-style local perturbation before the Muon update. MoMuSAM replaces the current clean gradient used to construct that perturbation with the previous momentum, removing one gradient evaluation.[3]

Does LLM Pre-Training Typically Occur at the Edge of Stability?

Does LLM Pre-Training Typically Occur at the Edge of Stability? ↗
Yuhang Cai · Haodong Wen · Haofeng Huang · Deyi Liu · Yiyuan Ma · Kaifeng Lyu
First posted 2026-10-27 · v1

Abstract only: persistent negative curvature makes local quadratic dynamics diverge at every positive learning rate (Edge of Convexity). After separating this effect, EoS is prevalent but not universal in pretraining up to 787M, disappearing at sufficiently small learning rates or batch sizes. This is Kaifeng Lyu author-subscription news.[5]

Positive features before normalization

Rethinking Attention with Performers ↗
Krzysztof Choromanski et al.
First posted 2020-09-30; read v4 (2022-11-19), §§2–3 and Appendix B.1
ICLR 2021 · important historical work in linear attention ↗

After random Fourier features (Rahimi–Recht, 2007) and Linear Transformers (2020), Performer targets the original softmax kernel rather than merely replacing it. Absorb d^{-1/4} into queries and keys, so a_ij=exp(q_iᵀk_j).[8][9]

Gaussian moment-generating identity; Lemma 1 [8]
ϕω(x)=exp⁡(ω⊤x−∥x∥2/2),ω∼N(0,I),E[ϕω(x)ϕω(y)]=ex⊤y\phi_\omega(x)=\exp(\omega^\top x-\|x\|^2/2),\quad\omega\sim N(0,I),\quad\mathbb E[\phi_\omega(x)\phi_\omega(y)]=e^{x^\top y}ϕω​(x)=exp(ω⊤x−∥x∥2/2),ω∼N(0,I),E[ϕω​(x)ϕω​(y)]=ex⊤y
Unbiased kernel estimator with positive features [8]
ϕ(x)=m−1/2(ϕω1(x),…,ϕωm(x)),a^ij=ϕ(qi)⊤ϕ(kj)≥0\phi(x)=m^{-1/2}(\phi_{\omega_1}(x),\ldots,\phi_{\omega_m}(x)),\quad \widehat a_{ij}=\phi(q_i)^\top\phi(k_j)\ge0ϕ(x)=m−1/2(ϕω1​​(x),…,ϕωm​​(x)),aij​=ϕ(qi​)⊤ϕ(kj​)≥0

Positivity avoids negative normalization sums. Orthogonal directions, while preserving Gaussian marginals, further reduce kernel-estimator variance. Kernel unbiasedness does not imply unbiasedness after taking a ratio.[8]

Reassociate products; no L×L attention matrix [8]
Y^=diag⁡(Qf(Kf⊤1))−1Qf(Kf⊤V),time=O(Lmdv)\widehat Y=\operatorname{diag}(Q_f(K_f^\top\mathbf1))^{-1}Q_f(K_f^\top V),\qquad \mathrm{time}=O(Lmd_v)Y=diag(Qf​(Kf⊤​1))−1Qf​(Kf⊤​V),time=O(Lmdv​)
Causal recurrence, fixed m and value dimension; Appendix B.1 [8]
St=St−1+ϕ(kt)vt⊤,zt=zt−1+ϕ(kt),y^t=ϕ(qt)⊤Stϕ(qt)⊤ztS_t=S_{t-1}+\phi(k_t)v_t^\top,\quad z_t=z_{t-1}+\phi(k_t),\qquad \widehat y_t={\phi(q_t)^\top S_t\over\phi(q_t)^\top z_t}St​=St−1​+ϕ(kt​)vt⊤​,zt​=zt−1​+ϕ(kt​),y​t​=ϕ(qt​)⊤zt​ϕ(qt​)⊤St​​
Boundary

Linear sequence-length cost fixes m. Required approximation accuracy depends on query/key norms and feature count; a fixed-size state does not preserve arbitrary exact softmax memory.[8]
