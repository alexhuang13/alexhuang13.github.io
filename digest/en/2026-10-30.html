Volume, breadth and order are distinct axes

Scaling Mathematical Proof Supervision from Structured Textbooks ↗

First posted 2026-10-29 · v1

TOME-Math retains chapters, proposition dependencies and local proof gaps. Unlike isolated QA, this supports reordering a fixed training set and separating data volume from source breadth.[2]

Experimental notation: identical examples, token budget and initialization within each subject–budget cell [2]
DB fixed:Shufflevs.Forward ⁣ ⁣− ⁣Gap\mathcal D_B\ \mathrm{fixed}:\quad \mathrm{Shuffle}\quad\mathrm{vs.}\quad\mathrm{Forward\!\! -\! Gap}DB​ fixed:Shufflevs.Forward−Gap

The table gives six-subject macro averages. At fixed 0.75M tokens, spreading supervision over 16 rather than eight books adds 1.6–4.8 points. Forward–Gap advances by chapter and prioritizes local proof gaps within stages.[2]

Boundary

Pass@16 measures at least one verifier-accepted solution in 16 draws, not single-draw accuracy. Finite-budget curves do not establish a universal extrapolatable power law.[2]

Equal pretraining loss need not mean equal transfer

Two technical articles · 2026-10-28 · overlap-window addition

H-Net compresses bytes through input-dependent chunks. Part I controls a one-stage comparison with BPE; Part II separates compute-to-validation loss from validation-to-downstream loss.[3][4]

Expository notation: architecture A can change both f and g; not a theorem [3,4]
Lval=fA(C),Leval=gA(Lval),Leval(C)=gA(fA(C))L_{\rm val}=f_A(C),\qquad L_{\rm eval}=g_A(L_{\rm val}),\qquad L_{\rm eval}(C)=g_A(f_A(C))Lval​=fA​(C),Leval​=gA​(Lval​),Leval​(C)=gA​(fA​(C))

Part II holds hierarchy and compression fixed while comparing entropy-proxy, frozen H-Net and end-to-end boundaries. End-to-end training gives the lowest validation BPB at all three tested budgets.[4]

Boundary

Multipliers concern fitted FLOPs curves, not wall time. BLT-like is a controlled adaptation, not a full reproduction of BLT. Ignoring proxy training and inference FLOPs favors the proxy baselines.[3][4]
