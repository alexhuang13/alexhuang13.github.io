MiniMax H3 receives SFT on aesthetically curated audio-video data, followed by PDMD for 50-to-six-step distillation. Instability of ordinary DMD is the authors’ empirical observation.[2]

Pipeline schematic; 8.3 is a step-count ratio, not wall-clock speedup [2]
W50→PDMDW6→ReFL  and  DPO  adaptersW6∗,506≈8.3W_{50}\xrightarrow{\mathrm{PDMD}}W_6\xrightarrow{\mathrm{ReFL\;and\;DPO\;adapters}}W_6^*,\qquad \frac{50}{6}\approx8.3W50​PDMD​W6​ReFLandDPOadapters​W6∗​,650​≈8.3

After distillation, separate ReFL and DPO low-rank adapters are trained and merged. ReFL differentiates rewards through current outputs; DPO uses offline preferences, with automated judgments calibrated by human comparisons.[2]

Which directions to forget, and how to parallelize them

Gated Linear Attention Transformers with Hardware-Efficient Training ↗
Songlin Yang · Bailin Wang · Yikang Shen · Rameswar Panda · Yoon Kim
First posted 2023-12-11; read v6 (2024-08-27), sections 2–5; ICML 2024
ICML 2024 · Important historical work on linear attention (official PMLR record checked 2026-11-02) ↗

Linear Transformers (2020) express attention as matrix recurrence; RetNet (2023) adds fixed decay. GLA selects decay directions from each input while retaining chunkwise computation.[4][5]

Single-head core: q and k are d_k-dimensional columns, v is d_v-dimensional, and S has shape d_k×d_v. Output gating, multi-head normalization and projection are omitted.[4]

Input-dependent rowwise forgetting; paper gates use a low-rank map, sigmoid and temperature [4]

Unrolled recurrence; an empty product is all ones; this is not softmax normalization [4]
ot=∑i=1t[qt⊤diag⁡ ⁣(∏r=i+1tαr)ki]vio_t=\sum_{i=1}^{t}\left[q_t^\top\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\prod_{r=i+1}^{t}\alpha_r\right)k_i\right]v_iot​=i=1∑t​[qt⊤​diag(r=i+1∏t​αr​)ki​]vi​

Implementation uses secondary tiling: half-precision matmuls between sub-chunks and full-precision log-space work within them. Materializing boundary states trades I/O and memory for sequence parallelism.[4]

Boundary

These are training perplexities, not test results. Linear sequence complexity still requires a d_k×d_v state per head and does not ensure speed on every GPU/batch size or lossless retention of arbitrary history.[4]
