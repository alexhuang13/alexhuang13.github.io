Brief | Learning to memorize random sequences faster

Meta-memorization and memorization scaling laws in transformers ↗
Alex Nguyen · Kenneth A. Norman · Gautam Reddy
First posted 2026-11-01 00:43 EDT · v1; delayed catch-up outside window

Random binary sequences remove semantic structure. Reported capacity grows roughly linearly with parameters, while memorization rate grows as a power of training time; prior memorization accelerates new-sequence storage. Mechanistic attribution and transfer to natural-language knowledge require further checks.[5]

REINFORCE (1992) uses full returns; TD(λ) combines bootstrapping and multistep returns. GAE applies this structure to advantages and combines it with TRPO for continuous control.[10][11]
