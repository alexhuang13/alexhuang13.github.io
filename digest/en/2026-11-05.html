Data reuse, turn selection and amortizing training

Dense-retrieval scaling · Turn-BoN · RL with notes · μP

Three main readings: retrieval early stopping with limited unique data, turn-level action selection at inference, and RL with fixed notes. Briefs cover scaling, Muon, technical reports and subscribed authors. The classic is μP.

Move Best-of-N before environment execution

On Turn-Level Best-of-N Sampling for Scaling LLM Agents ↗
Jalaj Bhandari · Dhawal Gupta · Hongli Zhan · Zhenting Wang · Varad Pimpalkhute · Zhengzhong Liu · Eric Xing · Rupesh Srivastava

Whole-trajectory resampling selects after completion. Here a stronger off-the-shelf model ranks candidate commands at every turn, and only the chosen command reaches the environment, avoiding branch rollback.[2]

How much should one update change features as width grows?

Tensor Programs V: Tuning Large Neural Networks via Zero-Shot Hyperparameter Transfer ↗
Greg Yang · Edward J. Hu · Igor Babuschkin · Szymon Sidor · Xiaodong Liu · David Farhi · Nick Ryder · Jakub Pachocki · Weizhu Chen · Jianfeng Gao
NeurIPS 2021; first arXiv posting and version read: v1, 2022-03-07
NeurIPS 2021 · Foundational work on hyperparameter transfer (proceedings checked 2026-11-05) ↗

Standard parametrization controls initialization scale without necessarily controlling feature updates. Tensor Programs’ infinite-width feature-learning analysis (2021) motivates μP; this paper turns it into small-model tuning followed by transfer.[21][22]
