Activation reuse, distillation compute and representation constraints

SAE scaling · OPD versus GRPO · Mechanistic Shaping · Flow Matching

Three readings ask when recycled activations approximate fresh data, where the compute bill for distillation starts, and whether sycophancy remains decodable after its behavior is reduced. Briefs cover test-time compute, data scaling and Muon in practice. The classic is Flow Matching.

How much fresh data can reused activations replace?

Scaling Laws for SAE Training Data ↗
Nikita Koriagin · Nikita Balagansky · Daniil Gavrilov
2026-11-05 05:00 EST · v1 first posted

Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) require extracting and storing base-model activations. Applying Muennighoff et al.’s (2023) data-reuse idea to reconstruction, distinguish unique activations U and dictionary width D at fixed exposure T. [1][2]

Empirical conversion: reuse initially approximates fresh data, then each exposure contributes less [1]
R=T/U−1,Neff=U[1+R∗(1−e−R/R∗)],R∗=cD−γR=T/U-1,\qquad N_{\rm eff}=U[1+R_*(1-e^{-R/R_*})],\qquad R_*=cD^{-\gamma}R=T/U−1,Neff​=U[1+R∗​(1−e−R/R∗​)],R∗​=cD−γ
EV is explained variance on held-out activations; terms represent insufficient width and effective data [1]
EV⁡≈E∞−AD−α−BNeff−β\operatorname{EV}\approx E_\infty-AD^{-\alpha}-BN_{\rm eff}^{-\beta}EV≈E∞​−AD−α−BNeff−β​

For Llama‑3.1‑8B layer 15 at D=32k and T=640M, the lower endpoint of near-saturation is U=40M for all three SAEs in Table 2, a 16-fold reduction in activation storage.

Boundary

Sparsity differs; compare reuse trends. Storage reduction does not imply end-to-end compute savings, and reconstruction variance does not measure feature interpretability. [1]

Distillation can be cheap; where did the teacher come from?

Unpacking the Compute Efficiency of Strong-to-Weak OPD against GRPO ↗
Zeyu Leo Liu · Yang Li · Bo Pang · Emre Can Acikgoz · Xuan-Phi Nguyen · Anurag Koul · Srijan Bansal · Erik Nijkamp · Shafiq Joty · Semih Yavuz
2026-11-05 13:47 EST · v1 first posted

The student generates its own rollouts. GRPO uses within-group outcome rewards; on-policy distillation (OPD) uses a frozen teacher and renormalizes both distributions over the student’s top 16 tokens at each step. [3]

T counts each run’s generated tokens; Nₛ and Nₜ count parameters. Teacher forwards add cost; shared prompts are omitted [3]
ℓOPD=DKL(π~s∥π~t),CGRPO≃10NsT,COPD≃(10Ns+2Nt)T\ell_{\rm OPD}=D_{\rm KL}(\widetilde\pi_s\|\widetilde\pi_t),\qquad C_{\rm GRPO}\simeq10N_s T,\qquad C_{\rm OPD}\simeq(10N_s+2N_t)TℓOPD​=DKL​(πs​∥πt​),CGRPO​≃10Ns​T,COPD​≃(10Ns​+2Nt​)T

On math alone, using a GRPO checkpoint of the same Qwen3‑4B‑Base as teacher matched the baseline with about 1/50 of GRPO’s FLOPs in the distillation stage. For multiple domains, the bill starts with training specialists, then merges them into a fresh Qwen3‑4B‑Base. [3]

Boundary

Matched scores are against the authors’ instance-mixed joint GRPO. External-teacher failures depend on the recipes tested, and FLOPs are not wall-clock time. [3]

After behavior changes, can a probe still decode the concept?

Shaping & Monitoring Models through their Internal Representations ↗
Satchel Grant · Michael Byun (blog); Thomas Fel et al. (paper)
Blog 2026-11-05; paper first posted 2026-11-04 14:29 EST · v1

Output rewards alone do not specify what a decision should rely on. The authors add a representation penalty to the task objective and distinguish concept expression from reliance. [4][5]

Minimization form of the framework: task loss plus a representation penalty [5]
L(θ)=R(θ)+λω(θ)\mathcal L(\theta)=\mathcal R(\theta)+\lambda\omega(\theta)L(θ)=R(θ)+λω(θ)
Illustration with fixed unit concept direction v: expression in activation z on the left, local sensitivity of readout s on the right
gv(z)=v⊤z+b,Dvs(z)=∇zs(z)⊤vg_v(z)=v^\top z+b,\qquad D_vs(z)=\nabla_zs(z)^\top vgv​(z)=v⊤z+b,Dv​s(z)=∇z​s(z)⊤v

The OLMo‑3‑7B sycophancy experiment adds a directional attribution penalty to DPO. The original fixed difference-of-means probe’s AUROC falls from 0.981 to 0.931; freely refitted linear probes still recover the signal at evaluated training steps. [4][5]

Boundary

Failure of a fixed probe does not show erasure, and successful refitting in this experiment does not ensure monitoring under distribution shift. [5]

Why does regressing conditional velocities learn the marginal flow?

Flow Matching for Generative Modeling ↗
Yaron Lipman · Ricky T. Q. Chen · Heli Ben-Hamu · Maximilian Nickel · Matt Le
ICLR 2023; first arXiv posting and version read: v1, 2022-10-06
ICLR 2023 · Historical work on continuous generative modeling (official record checked 2026-11-06) ↗

Neural ODEs (2018) formulate generation as continuous dynamics; score SDEs (2021) use noising paths to construct a training target. Flow Matching regresses velocities directly and samples a single path point during training. Draw data Y∼q, independent base noise X₀∼N(0,I), and uniform time t. [20][21]

Theorem 1: average conditional velocities under the posterior wherever the density is positive [20]
pt(x)=∫pt(x∣y) q(dy),ut(x)=E[ut(Xt∣Y)∣Xt=x]p_t(x)=\int p_t(x\mid y)\,q(dy),\qquad u_t(x)=\mathbb E[u_t(X_t\mid Y)\mid X_t=x]pt​(x)=∫pt​(x∣y)q(dy),ut​(x)=E[ut​(Xt​∣Y)∣Xt​=x]
Continuity equation: under the paper’s smoothness conditions, integrating conditional flux gives marginal flux [20]
∂tpt=−∇x⋅(ptut)\partial_t p_t=-\nabla_x\cdot(p_t u_t)∂t​pt​=−∇x​⋅(pt​ut​)
Regression interpretation of Theorem 2: the conditional residual has mean zero given (t,Xₜ), so the cross term vanishes [20]
E∥vθ(t,Xt)−U∥2=E∥vθ(t,Xt)−ut(Xt)∥2+E∥U−ut(Xt)∥2,U=ut(Xt∣Y)\mathbb E\|v_\theta(t,X_t)-U\|^2=\mathbb E\|v_\theta(t,X_t)-u_t(X_t)\|^2+\mathbb E\|U-u_t(X_t)\|^2,\quad U=u_t(X_t\mid Y)E∥vθ​(t,Xt​)−U∥2=E∥vθ​(t,Xt​)−ut​(Xt​)∥2+E∥U−ut​(Xt​)∥2,U=ut​(Xt​∣Y)
Conditional Gaussian OT path: each noise sample follows a straight line; terminal noise has scale σₘᵢₙ [20]
Xt=tY+[1−(1−σmin⁡)t]X0,U=Y−(1−σmin⁡)X0X_t=tY+[1-(1-\sigma_{\min})t]X_0,\qquad U=Y-(1-\sigma_{\min})X_0Xt​=tY+[1−(1−σmin​)t]X0​,U=Y−(1−σmin​)X0​
Integrate the learned ODE at sampling time [20]
dXtdt=vθ(t,Xt),X0∼N(0,I)\frac{dX_t}{dt}=v_\theta(t,X_t),\qquad X_0\sim\mathcal N(0,I)dtdXt​​=vθ​(t,Xt​),X0​∼N(0,I)
Boundary

Conditional OT does not imply marginal OT after mixing. With σₘᵢₙ>0 the ideal endpoint is data smoothed by small Gaussian noise; finite-step integration adds separate error. [20]
