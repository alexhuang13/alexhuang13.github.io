Async mismatch, production feedback and mixture-conditioned generation

REINFORCE Zero · Production RL · LLM Zoo · DeltaNet

Three readings separate two sources of asynchronous RL mismatch, construct learning signals from deployment logs, and generate weights conditioned on data mixtures. Eight briefs cover weight synchronization, autonomous post-training and inference compute allocation. The classic is DeltaNet.

Record the actual deployment sampling distribution

A Practical Recipe for Production RL ↗
Edward Yu · Raymond Feng
2026-11-06

Deployment μ may include temperature and top-p/top-k filtering, so it need not equal raw π at the same weights. The recipe collects SB trajectories per round and takes S updates of batch size B, keeping reference q fixed within a round and refreshing it afterward. [2]

Sampled-token KL at a fixed history, requiring full support under μ and normalized distributions [2]
DKL(π∥q)=Ea∼μ ⁣[q(a)−π(a)+π(a)log⁡(π(a)/q(a))μ(a)]D_{\rm KL}(\pi\|q)=\mathbb E_{a\sim\mu}\!\left[\frac{q(a)-\pi(a)+\pi(a)\log(\pi(a)/q(a))}{\mu(a)}\right]DKL​(π∥q)=Ea∼μ​[μ(a)q(a)−π(a)+π(a)log(π(a)/q(a))​]

The q−π terms sum to zero, preserving the same KL. The authors combine the estimator with score centering to reduce vocabulary computation and storage at the cost of sampling variance. [2]

Boundary

With top-p/top-k, some μ(a)=0 and the full-vocabulary identity fails. The authors explicitly acknowledge this bias; empirical stability is not an unbiasedness guarantee. [2]

Generate weights conditioned on the training mixture

A Night at the LLM Zoo: Data Conditioned Flow Matching for Generating Pretrained LLM Weights ↗
Scott Biggs · Bedionita Soro · Saumya Gupta · Bruno Andreis · Sung Ju Hwang
2026-11-04 15:16 EST · v1

Each of the 51M, 124M and 355M scales has 100 GPT-style models, branched from a common trunk onto different data mixtures. Weight deltas are compressed to 50 dimensions before learning mixture-conditioned generation. [3]

Notation: five-domain mixture p, Gram-PCA code z and conditional flow generator; this is not a power law for mixture-dependent loss [3]
p∈Δ4,z=P50(θ−θtrunk),z∼qϕ(z∣p)p\in\Delta^4,\qquad z=\mathcal P_{50}(\theta-\theta_{\rm trunk}),\qquad z\sim q_\phi(z\mid p)p∈Δ4,z=P50​(θ−θtrunk​),z∼qϕ​(z∣p)

On unseen mixtures, mean perplexity change is about 4.1–5.1%, versus 5.8–6.0% for retrieval. These quantify distortion relative to corresponding trained models, not language-modeling gains. [3]

Boundary

The PCA basis includes held-out models, so representation learning is transductive; mixtures are held out from generator training. This is not end-to-end generalization to entirely unseen weights and arbitrary mixtures. [3]

Why does writing residuals lead to a triangular solve?

Parallelizing Linear Transformers with the Delta Rule over Sequence Length ↗
Songlin Yang · Bailin Wang · Yu Zhang · Yikang Shen · Yoon Kim
NeurIPS 2024; first arXiv posting and version read: v1, 2024-06-10
NeurIPS 2024 · DeltaNet parallel algorithm (proceedings checked 2026-11-07) ↗

Linear attention (2020) accumulates key-value outer products, allowing collisions. DeltaNet (2021) reads the old value and writes its residual; this paper makes that recurrence GPU-friendly through chunking. State S∈R^(dᵥ×dₖ), key k, value v and query q satisfy ‖k‖₂=1 and 0<β<1. [12][13]

Delta rule: equivalent to one matrix-gradient step on ½‖Skₜ−vₜ‖² [12]

With unit keys: interpolate along the current key and preserve its orthogonal complement
Stkt=(1−βt)St−1kt+βtvt,Stx=St−1x(kt⊤x=0)S_tk_t=(1-\beta_t)S_{t-1}k_t+\beta_tv_t,\qquad S_tx=S_{t-1}x\quad(k_t^\top x=0)St​kt​=(1−βt​)St−1​kt​+βt​vt​,St​x=St−1​x(kt⊤​x=0)
Within-chunk pseudo-value recurrence; S₀ starts the chunk. Key Gram terms create causal dependencies
et=βt ⁣(vt−S0kt−∑j<tejkj⊤kt),St=S0+∑j≤tejkj⊤e_t=\beta_t\!\left(v_t-S_0k_t-\sum_{j<t}e_jk_j^\top k_t\right),\qquad S_t=S_0+\sum_{j\le t}e_jk_j^\topet​=βt​(vt​−S0​kt​−j<t∑​ej​kj⊤​kt​),St​=S0​+j≤t∑​ej​kj⊤​
Equivalent chunk derivation: K, V and E stack columns. Strictly upper-triangular U makes this a triangular solve, not a general dense inverse

Once E is available, matrix products yield chunk outputs and final state; this expresses the dependencies captured by the paper’s WY chunk algorithm [12]
