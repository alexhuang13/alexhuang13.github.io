What does fusion timing change when both token budgets are fixed?

Scaling Laws for Vision-Language Fusion ↗
Seng Pei Liew · Teppei Suzuki · Seiya Tokui
v1 submitted November 17, 2026 ET · November 18 announcement

Let Dₚ be text pretraining tokens, D fusion-stage tokens and R the caption fraction. The authors separate a power law in effective exposure from a distribution-shift term, using a fitted exchange rate w between token types. [1]

Paper’s empirical model. The modality m is caption or text, with σcap=−1 and σtxt=+1. The saturation amplitude ρ depends on the mixture ratio, while Φ builds up with training exposure.
Dcap=wcapDp+[R+wcap(1−R)]D,Lm=L0,m+AmDm−αm+σmBmρm(R)Φm(D)\mathcal D_{\mathrm{cap}}=w_{\mathrm{cap}}D_p+[R+w_{\mathrm{cap}}(1-R)]D,\qquad L_m=L_{0,m}+A_m\mathcal D_m^{-\alpha_m}+\sigma_m B_m\rho_m(R)\Phi_m(D)Dcap​=wcap​Dp​+[R+wcap​(1−R)]D,Lm​=L0,m​+Am​Dm−αm​​+σm​Bm​ρm​(R)Φm​(D)
At fixed budgets for both token types, the base exposure is independent of fusion timing, leaving timing effects in the shift term.
Ttxt=Dp+(1−R)D,Tcap=RD⟹Dcap=wcapTtxt+TcapT_{\mathrm{txt}}=D_p+(1-R)D,\quad T_{\mathrm{cap}}=RD\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\mathcal D_{\mathrm{cap}}=w_{\mathrm{cap}}T_{\mathrm{txt}}+T_{\mathrm{cap}}Ttxt​=Dp​+(1−R)D,Tcap​=RD⟹Dcap​=wcap​Ttxt​+Tcap​

Early fusion mixes from the start. Late fusion begins with text and then concentrates caption exposure. The experiment uses 56B total tokens and reports validation nats per token. [1]

Evidence boundary

At R=1, text capability collapses, so the text scaling fit is restricted to R≤0.82. Visual downstream scores do not consistently follow caption loss, which limits the table’s interpretation to the loss trade-off. [1]

Allocate five samples across five jointly trained LoRAs

Learning Stratified Policies for Inference-Time Objectives ↗
Mridul Mahajan · Xuezhou Zhang · Aldo Pacchiano
v1 submitted November 17, 2026 ET · November 18 announcement

A frozen backbone is shared across LoRA policies. One answer from each policy is drawn, and the population jointly optimizes the probability of at least one correct answer. The control draws five answers from one policy using the same five-sample advantage rule. [2]

Equivalent rewriting of the paper’s pass@k objective, with conditionally independent samples given x. A policy’s marginal benefit depends on how much probability the others leave uncovered.
J(θ1,…,θk)=Ex ⁣[1−∏i=1k(1−pi(x))],pi(x)=Pr⁡y∼πθi{r(x,y)=1}J(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_k)=\mathbb E_x\!\left[1-\prod_{i=1}^k(1-p_i(x))\right],\qquad p_i(x)=\Pr_{y\sim\pi_{\theta_i}}\{r(x,y)=1\}J(θ1​,…,θk​)=Ex​[1−i=1∏k​(1−pi​(x))],pi​(x)=y∼πθi​​Pr​{r(x,y)=1}
Equations (4) and (6). For binary rewards, LOO rewards only a unique success. GM draws i* uniformly among maximizers and updates only that winner. Mixed groups with several successes still yield a signal, but GM is biased for pass@k.
AiLOO=max⁡jrj−max⁡j≠irj,AiGM=1{i=i∗}(ri−1k∑jrj)A_i^{\mathrm{LOO}}=\max_j r_j-\max_{j\ne i}r_j,\qquad A_i^{\mathrm{GM}}=\mathbf1_{\{i=i^*\}}\left(r_i-\frac1k\sum_j r_j\right)AiLOO​=jmax​rj​−j=imax​rj​,AiGM​=1{i=i∗}​(ri​−k1​j∑​rj​)
Evidence boundary

Parentheses give standard errors over three seeds. LoRA rank is 32 and the output cap is 768 tokens. Rank-160 Single still scores 74.2 on Sudoku. The GSM control uses a pre-collapse checkpoint. The regional-linear expressivity theorem is an existence result, with LLM learning and budget gains supported empirically. [2]

Build RLVR from metabolism experiments in a CSV

Post-Training NVIDIA Nemotron to Increase Accuracy in Your Scientific Domain ↗
Gautham Dharuman · Xiaoyun Wang · Logan Ward · Geetika Gupta · Kibibi Moseley
Published November 16, 2026 · Added from the overlap window

Given a parent molecule and enzyme, the model submits the metabolite as SMILES. NeMo Gym connects the experimental reference to a reward server, and NeMo RL applies LoRA GRPO. The reward measures overlap between binary fingerprints of the predicted and reference molecules. [3]

Tanimoto similarity, with F denoting the molecular fingerprint. The same scorer can filter teacher traces before SFT.
r(m^,m)=∣F(m^)∩F(m)∣∣F(m^)∪F(m)∣r(\widehat m,m)=\frac{|F(\widehat m)\cap F(m)|}{|F(\widehat m)\cup F(m)|}r(m,m)=∣F(m)∪F(m)∣∣F(m)∩F(m)∣​
Evidence boundary

A threshold of 0.7 retains 1,123 teacher traces from 1,934 training prompts. Fingerprint similarity checks the final structure, while the chemical validity of each reasoning step remains separately unverified. The reported gains lack a sufficiently clear percentage definition, so this entry records the reproducible workflow and sample counts. [3]

Self-edited weights retain gains on a fixed held-out test

Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights ↗
Jiajun Fan · Neil He · Changyi Yang · et al.
v1 submitted November 16, 2026 ET · November 18 announcement

A 27B model inspects states, runs tests and edits its own weights. On 490 held-out math problems fixed in advance, the eighth session’s edit scores 63.8% versus 57.2% for the base. Some edits alter linear-attention decay. Another edit improves accuracy while increasing repetitive endings. [5]

Model scaling leaves kernel-attention quality gaps

The Kernel Reality Check: Benchmarking and Distilling Efficient Attention at Scale ↗
Fırat Öncel · Cem Subakan · Mirco Ravanelli · Çağatay Yıldız
v1 submitted November 17, 2026 ET · November 18 announcement

What does guidance strength change at each noise level?

Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance ↗
Jonathan Ho · Tim Salimans
NeurIPS 2021 DGMs workshop · First arXiv posting July 26, 2022 · Read arXiv v1
Highly cited in OpenAlex · Checked November 18, 2026 ↗

DDPM (2020) learns scores at different noise levels through noise prediction. Dhariwal–Nichol (2021) guides sampling using a noisy classifier gradient. Ho–Salimans combine conditional and unconditional noise estimates so one network supports a quality–diversity trade-off. [20]

During training, replace c with ∅ with probability pᵤ and otherwise retain it. The noise index λ is log SNR.
