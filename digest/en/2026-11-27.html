New tutorial: a shared policy learns to pool private information

Swarms, Flocks, Gangs: How to build, train, and evaluate multi-agent systems ↗
Ben Burtenshaw · Kashif Rasul
Blog: November 26, 2026; underlying paper is older; tutorial and reproduction results

Four Qwen3-0.6B agents each see one private integer; any correct complete sorted list wins. The paper was covered November 9; this is a new tutorial with preserved 75-update results. [1] [2]

Reward is specified in the post; the advantage formula summarizes its normalization rule across eight attempts per problem. All four agents share it. C counts generated team tokens; this is not causal credit assignment.

Journal extension: measurement quality beyond sample count

A measurement noise scaling law for cellular representation learning ↗
Gokul Gowri · Igor Sadalski · Dan Raviv · Peng Yin · Jonathan Rosenfeld · Allon M. Klein
Journal: November 26, 2026; preprint first posted March 4, 2025; compared v2: February 20, 2026

Auxiliary mutual information I(Y;Z) measures task-relevant information. Relative to February v2, the journal adds STATE and simulated correlated-noise/batch-effect analyses. The logarithmic law itself is older. [3] [4]

Paper’s independent scalar Gaussian explanatory model. U is variation unrelated to Y; E is channel noise. η corresponds to SNR after normalization, not raw sequencing depth.
X=Y+U,Z=ηX+E,Y∼N(μY,σY2), U∼N(0,σU2), E∼N(0,1)X=Y+U,\quad Z=\sqrt\eta X+E,\qquad Y\sim\mathcal N(\mu_Y,\sigma_Y^2),\ U\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma_U^2),\ E\sim\mathcal N(0,1)X=Y+U,Z=η​X+E,Y∼N(μY​,σY2​), U∼N(0,σU2​), E∼N(0,1)
Identity for the Gaussian model; a finite ceiling requires σᵤ²>0. The curve shape in high-dimensional non-Gaussian data is empirical, not a theorem for arbitrary neural networks.
I(Y;Z)=12log⁡21+η(σY2+σU2)1+ησU2,Imax⁡=12log⁡2σY2+σU2σU2I(Y;Z)=\frac12\log_2\frac{1+\eta(\sigma_Y^2+\sigma_U^2)}{1+\eta\sigma_U^2},\qquad I_{\max}=\frac12\log_2\frac{\sigma_Y^2+\sigma_U^2}{\sigma_U^2}I(Y;Z)=21​log2​1+ησU2​1+η(σY2​+σU2​)​,Imax​=21​log2​σU2​σY2​+σU2​​

Noise robustness and the high-SNR ceiling remain distinct. More samples need not substitute for better measurements; optimal budget allocation still requires a cost model. [3]

Evidence boundary

MI depends on the auxiliary task and estimator. The Gaussian derivation is neither a biological mechanism nor a universal representation-quality metric. No reproduction was performed. [3]

Monitor nearby capabilities while distilling a language

Distilling Kimi-K3.1-Pro into Qwen3.8-27B: Teaching a model a new language ↗
Zain Hasan · Sonny Khan
Blog first posted November 26, 2026

Off-policy text distillation trains Qwen3.8-27B LoRA on 3,834 teacher Lao translations plus 989 Thai translations. On 1,012 FLORES-200 devtest sentences, Lao chrF++ rises 5.5→44.3 (teacher 47.5); Thai on 300 sentences rises 46.7→47.8. Human-Lao-only training reaches 44.7 but collapses Thai to 5.3. Data sources and amounts differ, so this is not a strictly budget-matched causal ablation. [5]

Three algorithms for one semiseparable matrix

Transformers are SSMs: Generalized Models and Efficient Algorithms Through Structured State Space Duality ↗
Tri Dao · Albert Gu
ICML 2024; first posting and read version v1: May 31, 2024
OpenAlex: 2,416 citations (November 27, 2026) ↗

After S4 (2021) and input-dependent Mamba (2023), Dao and Gu (ICML 2024) constrain each head’s transition to a scalar times identity. Recurrence and attention then share one structured matrix. [6]

Notation for the paper’s single head: S₀=0 and aₜ is scalar. Coefficients may depend on input; the identities use the same coefficients for that input.

Expanding recurrence gives structured masked attention M=(QKᵀ)⊙L with Qᵢ=cᵢ and Kⱼ=bⱼ. Empty products equal one; there is no softmax.

Chunk-state identity reorganizing Section 6: local matrix products compute within-block outputs and final states; a cross-chunk scan propagates states; past contributions are added back.

Recurrence, quadratic attention and chunking compute the same operator up to floating-point effects. Chunk size changes hardware efficiency; core speedups do not establish end-to-end training speedups. [6]

Evidence boundary

Theory and algorithm read; implementation not run. Scalar transitions restrict SSD; arbitrary softmax Transformers are not thereby exactly replaceable by the same finite state. OpenAlex records 2,416 citations. [7]
